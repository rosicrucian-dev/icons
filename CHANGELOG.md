### Changed

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added

- The astrology set, 55 icons in the 24/outline style: the ten planets; the North and South Nodes and Chiron; the twelve signs; the conjunction, opposition, square, trine, quincunx and sextile; fire, earth, air and water; domicile, exaltation, detriment, fall and retrograde; the numerals 0–9; and the letters A, C, D, I and M for the angles.
- The `rosicrucian-icons`, `@rosicrucian-icons/react` and `@rosicrucian-icons/vue` packages, built as Heroicons builds its own.
